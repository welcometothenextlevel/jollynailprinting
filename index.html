How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

The machine does the difficult part. Your job is to turn up with clean nails and decide what you want them to look like.

  1. 01

    Prep your nails

    Come with clean, dry nails — old polish off, no hand cream or oil. The print needs a bare surface to hold onto. A base coat goes on first and needs a moment to set.

  2. 02

    Choose a design

    The screen shows all ten fingers. Tap each one and give it its own design, or apply one design to all ten. Want something personal? Scan the QR code on the machine and send a photo straight from your phone.

  3. 03

    Print, one finger at a time

    Rest a finger in the printing bay and hold still. The machine reads the shape and curve of the nail, then lays the design directly onto the surface. Lift out, next finger.

  4. 04

    Seal and go

    Finish with a top coat to lock the print in and give it its shine. Once it has set you are done — dry nails, straight out the door.

The machine

Built to print on the nails you
already have.

You do not need a fresh set or a particular length. The printer maps each nail before it prints, so it follows the curve of a natural nail as happily as a full acrylic tip.
