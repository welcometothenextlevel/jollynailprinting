Design families

Eight ways to leave the machine.

01

Classic white tips, coloured tips, micro French, thin outlines and single-line accents. Quiet enough for any workplace.

02

Wear & aftercare

Making a printed set last.

A print behaves like any good manicure. Sealed properly and treated sensibly, it holds its colour and detail for as long as the polish underneath it lasts.

  • Straight after Let the top coat set fully before you put your hands in a bag or pocket
  • First hour Keep away from hot water, washing up and hand sanitiser
  • Day to day Gloves for cleaning, and a fresh top coat every few days to keep the shine
  • Avoid Acetone-heavy hand products, and using your nails as tools
  • Removal Standard polish remover on natural nails. For gel or acrylic, see your usual technician
