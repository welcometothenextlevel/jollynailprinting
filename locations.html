Rules & regulations

Using the machine safely.

These apply at every Jolly machine, at every site. Using a machine means you accept them. They exist to keep the equipment clean, the queue moving and everyone’s nails intact.

Health & skin

Do not use a machine if you have a nail infection, a fungal condition, broken or bleeding skin around the nail, or a known allergy to nail polish, base coat or top coat. If you are unsure, speak to a doctor or a qualified nail technician first. Stop immediately and rinse with water if you feel any burning, stinging or irritation.

  1. Age and supervision

    Anyone under 16 must be accompanied and supervised by a parent or guardian for the whole session. Children must not be left alone at a machine at any time.

  2. Come with clean, dry nails

    Remove old polish, and wash and dry your hands before you start. Hand cream, oil, sanitiser residue and glitter left on the nail will stop the print from bonding — and a print that fails for this reason is not refundable.

  3. One person at a time

    Only the person printing should be at the machine. Wait behind the marked area if someone is mid-session — bumping the cabinet can ruin the print they have paid for.

  4. Hygiene

    Wipe the finger rest with the cleaning wipes provided before and after your session. Do not use a machine with unwashed hands, and please take your rubbish with you.

  5. Follow the on-screen instructions

    The touchscreen tells you when to place your finger, when to hold still and when to lift out. Moving early, forcing your finger into the bay or holding it at an angle is the most common cause of a smudged print.

  6. Payment

    Machines accept cash, card and contactless payment. Payment is taken before printing begins, and the price shown on screen is the price charged.

  7. Refunds

    If the machine takes payment and fails to print, you are entitled to a refund or a free reprint — contact us with the machine location, the date and the time. Refunds are not given for a change of mind about a design once printing has started, or for prints that fail because the nail was not prepared as instructed. This does not limit your rights under Australian Consumer Law.

  8. Images you upload

    If you send your own picture to the machine using its QR code, you confirm you have the right to use it. Do not upload anything offensive, unlawful, or protected by someone else’s copyright. We may refuse or remove uploads that break this rule.

  9. Looking after the machine

    Do not tamper with, lean on, tilt, strike or attempt to open any part of a machine, and do not insert anything into the printing bay other than a finger. Damage caused deliberately or through misuse will be pursued, and machines are monitored by the venue’s CCTV.

  10. Food, drink and belongings

    Keep drinks away from the machine. Personal belongings are your responsibility — neither Jolly Nail Printing nor the venue is responsible for items left behind.

  11. Venue rules apply too

    Each machine sits inside someone else’s venue. The venue’s trading hours, conduct rules and staff instructions apply while you are there, and take precedence.

  12. Reporting a problem

    If a machine is faulty, unclean or out of stock, tell us rather than the venue staff — they cannot service it. Include the location and the time, and we will attend to it.

Questions about any of the above, or something to report? Email jollynailprinting@gmail.com
